# Hospital Portal - Comprehensive Sub-Departments Structure

## Summary
Added **59 sub-departments** across all 14 standard departments for complete eye hospital operations coverage.

## Department Breakdown

### 1. STD_DOCTOR - Doctor (8 sub-departments)
Clinical medical specialties for ophthalmology:
- **RETINA** - Retina & Vitreous: Retinal surgery, vitreoretinal procedures, diabetic retinopathy
- **GLAUCOMA** - Glaucoma: Glaucoma diagnosis, laser therapy, surgical management
- **CORNEA** - Cornea: Corneal transplants, keratoconus, corneal diseases
- **CATARACT** - Cataract: Cataract surgery, phacoemulsification, IOL implantation
- **PEDIATRIC** - Pediatric Ophthalmology: Pediatric eye care, squint surgery, amblyopia treatment
- **OCULOPLASTY** - Oculoplasty: Eyelid surgery, orbital surgery, cosmetic procedures
- **NEURO_OPHTH** - Neuro-Ophthalmology: Optic nerve disorders, visual pathway diseases
- **UVEA** - Uvea & Immunology: Uveitis, ocular immunology, inflammatory eye diseases

### 2. STD_OPTOMETRIST - Optometrist (4 sub-departments)
Vision testing and correction services:
- **REFRACTION** - Refraction Services: Vision testing, prescription determination, automated refraction
- **CONTACT_LENS** - Contact Lens Clinic: Contact lens fitting, RGP lenses, specialty lenses
- **LOW_VISION** - Low Vision Aids: Visual rehabilitation, low vision devices, magnification aids
- **ORTHOPTICS** - Orthoptics: Eye muscle evaluation, binocular vision assessment, strabismus screening

### 3. STD_COUNSELOR - Counselor (3 sub-departments)
Patient counseling and guidance:
- **PRE_SURGERY** - Pre-Surgery Counseling: Surgical procedure explanation, consent, pre-op instructions
- **ADMISSION** - Admission Counseling: Admission procedures, documentation, patient guidance
- **INSURANCE_COUNSEL** - Insurance Counseling: Insurance coverage verification, claim guidance, cashless processing

### 4. STD_FRONT_OFFICE - Front Office (4 sub-departments)
Patient-facing administrative services:
- **RECEPTION** - Reception Desk: Patient registration, appointment scheduling, general inquiries (24x7)
- **APPOINTMENTS** - Appointment Management: Online/phone bookings, follow-up scheduling, appointment coordination
- **PATIENT_RELATIONS** - Patient Relations: Patient feedback, complaint resolution, satisfaction surveys
- **TELEHEALTH** - Telehealth Coordination: Virtual consultation setup, video call support, remote patient management

### 5. STD_IMAGING - Scan/Imaging (4 sub-departments)
Diagnostic imaging services:
- **OCT** - OCT Imaging: Optical coherence tomography scans (24x7)
- **FUNDUS** - Fundus Photography: Retinal imaging and documentation (24x7)
- **BSCAN** - B-Scan Ultrasound: Ocular ultrasound imaging
- **PERIMETRY** - Visual Field Testing: Perimetry and visual field analysis

### 6. STD_NURSE - Nurse (OT Management) (5 sub-departments)
Nursing care across patient journey:
- **PRE_OP** - Pre-Operative Care: Patient preparation, pre-op assessment, vitals monitoring (24x7)
- **POST_OP** - Post-Operative Care: Recovery monitoring, post-op care, discharge instructions (24x7)
- **OT_NURSING** - Operation Theatre Nursing: Surgical assistance, OT management, sterile technique (24x7)
- **IPD_NURSING** - In-Patient Department: Inpatient care, medication administration, ward management (24x7)
- **EMERGENCY_NURSING** - Emergency Nursing: Emergency response, trauma care, critical situations (24x7)

### 7. STD_JUNIOR_DOCTOR - Junior Doctor (3 sub-departments)
Resident physician services:
- **RESIDENT_OPD** - Resident OPD: Initial patient screening, preliminary diagnosis, resident consultations
- **FOLLOW_UP** - Follow-Up Clinic: Post-operative follow-ups, routine check-ups, treatment monitoring
- **TRAINING** - Training & Supervision: Medical education, hands-on training, supervised procedures

### 8. STD_PHARMACY - Pharmacy (4 sub-departments)
Medication dispensing and management:
- **OUTPATIENT_PHARMA** - Outpatient Pharmacy: Retail pharmacy for outpatients, prescription dispensing
- **INPATIENT_PHARMA** - Inpatient Pharmacy: Ward medication supply, IV preparation, inpatient prescriptions (24x7)
- **DRUG_INFO** - Drug Information Center: Medication counseling, drug interactions, pharmacovigilance
- **COMPOUNDING** - Compounding Pharmacy: Custom formulations, eye drops preparation, sterile compounding

### 9. STD_OPTICAL - Optical (3 sub-departments)
Eyewear sales and services:
- **SPECTACLE_SALES** - Spectacle Sales: Eyeglass frames, lens selection, prescription glasses
- **LENS_LAB** - Lens Laboratory: Lens cutting, edging, fitting, customization
- **SUNGLASSES** - Sunglasses & Accessories: Protective eyewear, sunglasses, eye accessories

### 10. STD_INSURANCE - Insurance (3 sub-departments)
Insurance claim processing:
- **CASHLESS** - Cashless Claims: Pre-authorization, cashless approvals, TPA coordination
- **REIMBURSEMENT** - Reimbursement Claims: Post-treatment claims, documentation support, reimbursement processing
- **VERIFICATION** - Insurance Verification: Coverage verification, policy validation, eligibility checks

### 11. STD_BILLING - Billing Management (4 sub-departments)
Financial transactions and billing:
- **OPD_BILLING** - OPD Billing: Outpatient billing, consultation fees, diagnostic charges
- **IPD_BILLING** - IPD Billing: Inpatient billing, surgery packages, discharge billing (24x7)
- **ACCOUNTS** - Accounts Receivable: Payment collection, outstanding dues, financial reporting
- **CASHIER** - Cashier Services: Cash handling, payment processing, refunds (24x7)

### 12. STD_INVENTORY - Inventory (4 sub-departments)
Supply chain and asset management:
- **MEDICAL_STORES** - Medical Stores: Surgical consumables, medical equipment, sterile supplies (24x7)
- **PROCUREMENT** - Procurement: Vendor management, purchase orders, contract negotiation (Requires Approval)
- **WAREHOUSE** - Warehouse Management: Stock management, storage, distribution logistics
- **ASSET_MGMT** - Asset Management: Equipment tracking, maintenance scheduling, asset depreciation

### 13. STD_ADMIN - Admin Management (5 sub-departments)
Hospital administration and support:
- **HR** - Human Resources: Recruitment, payroll, employee relations, performance management
- **IT_SUPPORT** - IT Support: Technical support, system maintenance, software troubleshooting
- **HOUSEKEEPING** - Housekeeping: Facility cleaning, hygiene maintenance, sanitation (24x7)
- **SECURITY** - Security Services: Facility security, access control, surveillance (24x7)
- **MAINTENANCE** - Maintenance & Engineering: Equipment repair, facility maintenance, electrical/plumbing services (24x7)

### 14. STD_LABORATORY - Laboratory (4 sub-departments)
Laboratory testing services:
- **PATHOLOGY** - Pathology Lab: Blood tests, cytology, histopathology, tissue analysis
- **MICROBIOLOGY** - Microbiology: Culture tests, infection screening, antimicrobial sensitivity
- **BIOCHEMISTRY** - Biochemistry: Blood chemistry, metabolic panels, glucose testing
- **SEROLOGY** - Serology & Immunology: Antibody testing, viral markers, immunological tests

## Department Types Distribution
- **Clinical**: 20 sub-departments (Doctor specialties, Optometrist, Junior Doctor, Nursing)
- **Diagnostics**: 8 sub-departments (Imaging, Laboratory)
- **Administrative**: 31 sub-departments (Front Office, Counselor, Pharmacy, Optical, Insurance, Billing, Inventory, Admin)

## 24x7 Operations
14 sub-departments operate round-the-clock:
- Reception Desk (Front Office)
- OCT Imaging, Fundus Photography (Imaging)
- All 5 Nursing sub-departments (Pre-Op, Post-Op, OT, IPD, Emergency)
- Inpatient Pharmacy
- IPD Billing, Cashier Services
- Medical Stores
- Housekeeping, Security, Maintenance

## Approval Requirements
1 sub-department requires approval workflows:
- **Procurement** (Inventory) - for vendor contracts and purchase orders

## Database Schema
All sub-departments include:
- `id` (UUID, primary key)
- `tenant_id` (UUID, multi-tenancy)
- `parent_department_id` (UUID, links to standard department)
- `department_level = 2` (hierarchical level)
- `department_code` (unique identifier)
- `department_name` (display name)
- `department_type` (Clinical/Diagnostics/Administrative)
- `description` (detailed services)
- `status` ("Active")
- `is_24x7` (boolean, operational hours)
- `requires_approval` (boolean, workflow requirement)
- Standard audit fields (created_at, updated_at)
